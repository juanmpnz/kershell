## Rendering and limits

This is a hybrid 2.5D scene, not a fully modelled walkable building. Three.js
renders the art on a background plane and separates four projective exhibit
layers at different depths. A real perspective camera advances slightly with
scroll and responds to the pointer; the layers exhibit relative parallax.
The photographic architecture and reflections themselves remain baked into the
art plate. Preserve this distinction when discussing future additions.

The local WebP is 192 KB. Both the HTML poster and Three.js use the same URL,
so the browser can reuse it. No external HDRs, room-environment generator,
metallic ribbons, bloom or shadow maps. Limit rendering to 30 FPS and DPR 1.5.
Pause outside the hero, when hidden, or via the explicit pause button. Dispose
textures, materials, geometries, observers and event listeners on unmount.

Mobile, reduced motion, failed texture/import/renderer initialization and context
loss retain the static architecture and HTML. Hero title stays white and centered.
Project links include all four products, including Campos on smaller screens.
